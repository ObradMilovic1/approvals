What each service covers

Bookkeeping and BAS: keeping your Xero file up to date, reconciling bank and card accounts, processing supplier bills, and preparing and lodging BAS and IAS. Payroll: pay runs, Single Touch Payroll reporting, and paying super with each pay run. Tax returns: preparing and lodging income tax returns for the entities in our quote. Company and SMSF set-up: preparing the documents and registrations in our quote. SMSF annual compliance: annual accounts, member statements and the SMSF annual return, and sending the file to an independent approved SMSF auditor.

What is not included

Legal or financial product advice, audits (the SMSF audit is done by an independent auditor), and anything not in our quote. We quote extra work before we start.

What we need from you

The documents on the checklist we send for each service, prompt answers to our questions, and notice of any change in your circumstances. You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the information you give us, for keeping records for 5 years, and for your own decisions. You make all payments from your own accounts; we do not move your money.

Fees

Our fees are as set out in our quote. If the work turns out to be materially different from what was quoted, we will tell you and agree a revised fee before continuing.

Our professional obligations

Tax and BAS services are provided by registered tax and BAS agents under the Tax Agent Services Act 2009 and the Code of Professional Conduct. If we find that something lodged is wrong, we must tell you and may need to tell the ATO. For company and SMSF set-ups we must verify identity under the AML/CTF Act.

Software and AI

We use Xero and other software, including AI tools, for routine work such as coding transactions and drafting emails. A qualified person reviews everything before it is lodged with the ATO or sent to you.

Privacy, records and ending

We handle your information under the Privacy Act 1988 and keep our working papers for 5 years. Either of us can end this engagement with 30 days' written notice; we will hand over your records once fees are paid. This letter continues for future years until it is ended.
